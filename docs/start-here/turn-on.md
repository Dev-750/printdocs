1. Plug in Printer

![printer cord]

Locate a 120VAC receptacle. Take the the power cord extending from the back of the printer, and line-up the three prongs with the receptacle in push it in.

![pluged in]

2. Power it on

![power switch] 

Locate the 3-position power switch on the back of the printer. it is in an up-facing position.

![power switch on] 

Flip the switch to either side to turn on the printer.
